About

Think like an attacker. Act like a guardian.

The mindset I bring to every engagement.

Specializing in VAPT & Application Security with expertise in identifying and exploiting vulnerabilities before attackers do. I secure the stack so your users sleep peacefully.

I am a Security Analyst at Digit Insurance with ~2.10 years of hands-on experience in cybersecurity across two roles — as a Security Testing Engineer at ConvertCart and currently at Digit Insurance. My work spans web, API, and mobile application security — leveraging industry-standard tools such as Burp Suite, Nmap, Postman, and Nessus to identify and remediate critical security risks before they can be exploited.

I hold the Burp Suite Certified Practitioner (BSCP) from PortSwigger, the Certified Red Team Professional (CRTP) from Altered Security, and the Certified Ethical Hacker (CEH) from EC-Council — with hands-on expertise in Active Directory attacks, lateral movement, and privilege escalation, bringing a real-world offensive perspective to securing enterprise environments.

I work closely with development and operations teams to integrate security practices throughout the software development lifecycle, ensuring vulnerabilities are addressed proactively rather than reactively. My approach balances technical depth with clear communication — translating complex security findings into actionable insights for both technical and non-technical stakeholders.

Beyond client work, I actively hunt for real-world vulnerabilities through bug bounty programs — earning a Hall of Fame recognition and a rewarded bounty through responsible disclosure. I've reached Grandmaster rank on HackTheBox with a 12-week practice streak, and Sage level on TryHackMe, reflecting consistent, self-driven practice across web, network, and Active Directory challenges.

I'm also genuinely curious about what happens below the application layer — malware analysis, reverse engineering, and binary exploitation are ongoing personal pursuits I explore through CTFs and hands-on practice with tools like Ghidra and IDA. It's not my primary focus, but it sharpens how I think about security as a whole.

In my day-to-day work, I use agentic AI tools to accelerate recon, triage findings, and speed up reporting — treating AI as a genuine productivity multiplier, not just a novelty, in a field that moves as fast as this one does.
